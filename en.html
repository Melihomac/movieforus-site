Matching

Two ways to a match

MovieForUs proposes people on their film choices, not their photos. Either way is enough:

  1. 1

    Shared taste

    You have both decided on at least 8 of the same films and agree on most of them.

  2. 2

    Shared hearts

    You have hearted the same 5 films. With a favourite actor in common, 3 are enough.

  3. 3

    Always nearby

    Everyone is within 300 km. Distances are shown rounded to 5 km.
